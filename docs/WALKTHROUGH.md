# Fictional end-to-end contract example

All material is invented. A test character repaired a paper lantern, saw a tide chart, and has an unresolved paper-bridge puzzle. Snapshot fixture-v1 contains only these fictional sources.

Scene 1: a bridge reaches a dry harbor; lanterns dim. Goal obstruction and uncertain control move simulated curiosity toward unease. Scene 2 receives that scene and affect, and may leave the bridge unresolved. No positive ending is required.

The original dream is saved with `materialKind:fictional`, `origin:dream`, `isRealityFact:false`. Wake records preserve a fragment and separate decaying residue, and may have zero associations. A later lantern cue proposes this dream as a dream recall candidate, never as evidence of a real harbor visit.

A later recollection or imagined continuation creates a child entry. It cannot alter either original scene. Cherishing changes neither cue access nor residue strength. Withdrawal of the lantern source removes the whole dependent dream and descendants, suppresses cached recall IDs, and blocks resume. This walkthrough describes expected behavior, not an executed generation.
